## Response Times

We aim to respond to community contributions within the following timeframes:

| Type | Initial Response | Resolution Target |
|------|------------------|-------------------|
| Security Issues | 48 hours | ASAP (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) |
| Bug Reports | 3-5 business days | 2 weeks |
| Feature Requests | 1 week | Evaluated quarterly |
| Pull Requests | 3-5 business days | 2 weeks |
| Questions | 1 week | N/A |

## Decision Making

- **Minor changes** (typos, docs, small fixes): Approved by any maintainer
- **Feature additions**: Require approval from lead maintainer
- **Breaking changes**: Require discussion in GitHub Issues first
- **Architecture changes**: Require RFC process (coming soon)

## Becoming a Maintainer

We welcome new maintainers! To be considered:

1. Be an active contributor (3+ merged PRs)
2. Demonstrate understanding of the project goals
3. Show consistent, quality contributions
4. Be invited by existing maintainers
